# Product

## Register

product

## Users
AI Engineers and VIP Coders who need to build advanced, complex system prompts.

## Product Purpose
A spatial, node-based prompt compiler tool designed to make advanced prompt engineering visual, minimal, and highly logical.

## Brand Personality
Minimal, easy, and reasonable. Visually inspired by node-based editors like ComfyUI.

## Anti-references
Generic "AI" template designs, overused AI aesthetic tropes.

## Design Principles
1. **Spatial Clarity**: The canvas and node routing must be legible and unobtrusive, prioritizing the workflow over decoration.
2. **Minimalist Utility**: Features should be easy and reasonable; avoid bloated interfaces in favor of a clean, focused engineering environment.
3. **Authentic Tooling**: The interface must feel like a genuine, high-end IDE rather than a generic wrapper.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Artistic aesthetic focus, maintaining high visual quality and contrast while serving advanced technical users.
